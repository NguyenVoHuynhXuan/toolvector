## Where Each Studio Wins in Practice

### 1. Live Guest Simplicity & Show Polish (Winner: StreamYard)
Non-technical executives and podcast guests join a **StreamYard Greenroom** on desktop or mobile in two clicks with zero app downloads or Chrome-permission headaches. Hosts switch cleanly between 16+ TV-style scenes, pop YouTube/LinkedIn viewer comments onto the stage, and let guests **simulcast simultaneously to their own channels (Guest Destinations)**—detailed further in our [StreamYard 2026 Review](/blog/streamyard-review/).

### 2. Post-Production Transcript Editing (Winner: Riverside.fm)
If 95% of your output is **pre-recorded** episodes where you want to delete filler words ("um", "ah") and cut 30-second tangents directly by highlighting text in the AI transcript, Riverside’s editor saves hours in post.

### 3. OBS / Gaming Encoder Relay (Winner: Restream)
If you already mix complex gameplay scenes locally inside OBS Studio and simply need an inexpensive cloud RTMP splitter to send one feed to Twitch, Kick, and YouTube simultaneously, Restream remains a fast, no-frills relay.
