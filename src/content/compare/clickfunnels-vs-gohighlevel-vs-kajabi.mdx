- **GoHighLevel** is an **agency operations and local lead-gen CRM** designed for marketing agencies selling automated SMS, missed-call text-back, and white-label software to brick-and-mortar businesses.

## Category-by-Category Deep Dive

### 1. Funnel Conversion Rate & Average Order Value (Winner: ClickFunnels)
When sending paid Meta, YouTube, or Google Ads to an offer, your landing page speed and checkout friction determine whether your campaign scales or bleeds cash. **ClickFunnels** includes **2-step smart order forms** (capturing email/phone on Step 1 for automated cart abandonment recovery), **checkbox Order Bumps** right above the buy button, and **true 1-click post-purchase OTO Upsells & Downsells** powered by PaymentsAI, Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Klarna/Affirm BNPL. In contrast, Kajabi lacks granular multi-step split testing, and GoHighLevel requires significantly more manual styling to match ClickFunnels’ conversion polish.

### 2. Agency Client Management & SMS Automation (Winner: GoHighLevel)
If your agency runs Facebook lead forms for dentists, roofers, or med-spas and bills retainers for appointment booking, **GoHighLevel** wins cleanly. Its missed-call text-back, native Google Business chat sync, and white-label SaaS sub-accounts allow an agency on the **$297/mo Unlimited** plan to host dozens of local businesses under custom agency branding.

### 3. Course & Community UX (Tie: ClickFunnels vs. Kajabi)
Kajabi has long been loved by solo educators for its clean course player and mobile student app. However, **ClickFunnels’ 2026 course suite** (3 courses on Launch, 6 on Scale, 10 on Optimize, 20 on Dominate) closes the gap while adding **in-course upgrade paywalls** and seamless connection to VSL and webinar funnels—plus **50,000 to 1.2M monthly emails** included without extra autoresponder bills.
