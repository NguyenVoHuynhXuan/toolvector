## 1. What Is Beam Eye Tracker?

Beam Eye Tracker is eye- and head-tracking software developed by Eyeware, a Swiss eye-tracking technology company. Its main purpose is to turn a standard webcam or compatible phone into an input device for head and eye tracking.

The software is primarily aimed at PC gaming, particularly genres where looking around the game world is useful. The official website highlights flight simulators, racing simulators, trucking, farming, combat and space games among its supported use cases.

Instead of purchasing separate tracking hardware, users can use:
- A built-in laptop webcam
- An external USB webcam
- A compatible smartphone used as a webcam

Beam currently supports hundreds of PC games, including titles such as Star Citizen, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, Euro Truck Simulator 2, DCS World, War Thunder, F1 25, Farming Simulator 25, BeamNG.drive and Assetto Corsa.

## 2. How Does Beam Eye Tracker Work?

Getting started is designed to be relatively simple.

1. **Install Beam**: Install the Beam Eye Tracker application and follow its guided setup.
2. **Choose Your Camera**: You can select a built-in or external webcam, or use a phone as a webcam. Beam says a standard 720p webcam can provide a cost-effective setup at 30 FPS. It recommends a camera with a regular-to-narrow field of view, roughly 50–80 degrees, for optimal tracking.
3. **Position the Camera**: The recommended position is generally at the top center of your main gaming display. The software can compensate when the camera isn’t perfectly centered, and users can also place the camera below the display if that works better for your setup.
4. **Calibrate and Start Playing**: After completing the guided setup, launch a supported game. Most games are designed to work automatically, although some titles require a quick in-game setting change. Beam provides individual setup guides for supported games.

## 3. Key Beam Eye Tracker Features

### Webcam-Based Eye Tracking
The core feature is AI-powered eye tracking using a webcam. Instead of buying dedicated eye-tracking hardware, your existing camera can be used to provide tracking input. This makes Beam particularly interesting for gamers who already own a webcam and want to experiment with eye tracking without building a specialized hardware setup.

### 6DoF Head Tracking
Beam also provides 6DoF (Six Degrees of Freedom) head tracking. The system tracks movements including Yaw, Pitch, Roll, Forward movement, Sideways movement, and Vertical movement. Head tracking can be used on its own or combined with eye tracking. For simulation games, this makes looking around a cockpit or checking mirrors more natural.

### Direct Game Camera Control
Games can receive the tracking input directly without requiring separate third-party software running alongside it. This is particularly relevant because older webcam tracking setups could involve several pieces of software between the camera and the game.

### Use Your Phone as a Webcam
You don’t necessarily need to buy a webcam. Beam supports using a phone as a webcam, including built-in webcam functionality on compatible Android devices and Apple’s Continuity Camera on supported iOS devices. For Face ID-enabled iPhones and iPads, Beam also provides an iOS app extension that can offload processing and use the device’s TrueDepth camera.

### Productivity and Streaming Tools
Beam isn’t limited to gaming camera control. It also includes:
- **Multi-Screen Pointer**: Moves the cursor to the monitor you’re looking at.
- **Mouse Jump**: Moves the cursor to your gaze position using a hotkey.
- **Eye Tracking Overlay**: Shows viewers where you’re looking during gaming streams, which can be captured through OBS and other recording software.

## 5. Webcam Requirements & Setup Considerations

One of the biggest advantages of Beam is that you don’t necessarily need dedicated tracking hardware. Most users can start with a standard 720p webcam, either built into a laptop or connected externally. 

For smoother tracking, Beam recommends prioritizing frame rate over resolution (e.g., 60 FPS is better than higher-resolution 30 FPS if you have to choose). The company recommends a field of view around 50–80 degrees. Very wide-angle webcams can introduce inaccuracies or tracking overshoot.

### Does Beam Eye Tracker Work With Glasses?
Yes. Most users wearing glasses should be able to use the tracker without major problems. However, strong prescriptions, lens glare, or thick frames can reduce tracking accuracy in certain situations. If the camera has trouble seeing the eyes from above the monitor, Beam suggests trying a position below the screen.

### OS Support & Privacy
Beam is available as a standalone application for Windows, with Linux and macOS support currently in public beta. Processing happens locally on the user’s device and Beam does not generate or store biometric data, which is great for privacy.
