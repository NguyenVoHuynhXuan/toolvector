### What is Aimfox?

Aimfox is an AI-powered LinkedIn outreach and lead generation platform designed for sales teams, growth experts, and go-to-market (GTM) agencies. Trusted by more than 10,000 outreach experts, the software addresses the fundamental scalability issue of LinkedIn marketing.

While traditional tools only automate the single account you log in with, Aimfox allows users to connect their own accounts (Bring Your Own Accounts) or utilize a native infrastructure to rent external, fully verified LinkedIn profiles directly through their ecosystem. This shifts LinkedIn outreach from a restricted, individual effort into a predictable, multi-channel-style pipeline that scales exactly like cold email.

### Core Features & Real Benefits Exploded

<Image src={aimfoxPic2} alt="Aimfox Core Features" class="prose-img" />

- **Native Profile Rentals (Outreach Avatars)**: Instead of sourcing fragile, unverified accounts from shady third-party vendors, Aimfox lets you natively rent and customize external LinkedIn profiles. This allows you to scale your outreach to thousands of leads per day safely. If an account hits a limit, your primary business identity remains untouched, allowing your outbound engine to run continuously on autopilot.
- **The Centralized Control Tower (Unibox)**: A single, consolidated master inbox that pulls in every message, connection request, and reply across all connected or rented profiles. Your SDRs or inbox managers do not have to waste hours logging into different browser profiles or managing separate proxy connections. One single manager can seamlessly handle conversations for multiple sales reps from a single screen.
- **Humanized Conversion Suite**: Built-in tools for AI-driven customization, native voice messaging, variable placeholders, and file attachments. It breaks through the automated inbox noise. Prospects receive voice notes and tailored messages that feel completely organic, resulting in higher reply rates compared to rigid, robotic templates.
- **Geo-Based Safety & Smart Scheduling**: Automatic smart timezone adjustments, auto-translation capabilities, and built-in geo-based IP/proxy selection. It keeps your automated actions under the radar of LinkedIn's security algorithms. Messages land exactly when the prospect is active in their local time zone, and the localized proxy setup prevents suspicious login flags.
