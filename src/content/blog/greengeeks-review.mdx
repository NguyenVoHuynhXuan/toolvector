- **Zero "Noisy Neighbor" Spikes via LVE Containers:** Every account runs inside an isolated **CloudLinux Lightweight Virtualized Environment (LVE)** with dedicated vCPU, RAM, and Disk I/O allocations, paired with proactive AI WAF rules and real-time malware scanning—meaning another customer’s traffic spike or compromised plugin on the same physical chassis cannot crash or infect your domain.
