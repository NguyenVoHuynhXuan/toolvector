## 5 Things to Know Before Buying Runable

1. **It is a deliverable-producing agent, not a conversational chatbot:** Runable does not just answer questions; it generates finished assets—websites with live databases, cross-platform React Native code, pitch decks, Notion-style reports, and paid ad campaigns.
2. **Pricing operates on credits with opaque deduction rates:** Rather than charging per user seat or per document, Runable deducts credits for each generation, revision, and model query. Users frequently report that credit burn rates feel unusual, variable by AI model, and hard to anticipate in advance.
3. **There is a genuinely usable free tier:** Runable provides **1,500 free credits every day**. However, daily credits do not roll over to the next day; you either use them or lose them.
4. **The company is very young:** Founded in 2025 in Bengaluru, Runable operated with a lean team of approximately 15 people when it secured its **$21 million Series A** in August 2026. Platform stability and customer support response times carry higher risk than mature enterprise tools.
5. **The strongest performance numbers are vendor self-reported:** Claims of a **92.1% score on GAIA**, 1.5M+ active businesses, and a 5.0/5 Trustpilot rating originate directly from company press releases or tiny review samples (only 5 reviews). Treat these metrics as promotional claims until independently reproduced.

## What Is Runable?

Runable is a general-purpose AI agent headquartered in Bengaluru, India. It was founded in 2025 by Umesh Kumar with the mission of turning high-level business ideas into finished digital products and continuous marketing campaigns through natural language prompts.

<Image src={runablePic1} alt="Runable AI workspace interface showing the multi-format generation canvas and agent workflows" class="article-body-img" />

*Runable workspace: transitioning from natural language prompts to multi-format deliverables, interactive canvas edits, and automated workflows.*

The platform is structured around three foundational verbs:
- **Build:** Generates consumer-facing deliverables (websites, mobile applications, slides, spreadsheets, reports, videos, podcasts, and graphics).
- **Run:** Automates operational workflows, scheduled background jobs, meeting summaries (Notetaker), and chat-based assistant actions (RunClaw).
- **Grow:** Launches and optimizes marketing campaigns (paid ads across Meta, Google, and ChatGPT Ads, SEO audits, social media posting, and voice AI outreach).

In August 2026, Runable announced a **$21 million Series A funding round** co-led by **Susquehanna Venture Capital** and **Nexus Venture Partners**, with participation from Together Fund and Array VC. Company announcements claimed that over 1.5 million businesses have adopted the tool, generating over $2 million in annualized recurring revenue (ARR) within weeks of enabling paid tiers.

## Detailed Pricing: How the Credit System Works

Runable replaces traditional per-seat licensing with a **hybrid credit consumption model**:

### Plan Structure Breakdown

| Tier | Price (Monthly) | Price (Annual Billing) | Credit Allowance | Best Suited For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | **$0** | **$0** | **1,500 credits / day** (~45,000/mo) | Casual creators, testing deliverables, lightweight tasks |
| **Pro** | **$20/mo** | **$180/yr** *($15/mo - 25% off)* | **1,500/day + 25,000 monthly credits** (~70,000/mo) | Solo founders, active builders, weekly websites/decks |
| **Max** | **$100/mo** | **$840/yr** *($70/mo - 30% off)* | **1,500/day + 150,000 monthly credits** (~195,000/mo) | Agencies, heavy video generation, multi-channel Grow ads |

*Credit Pack Add-ons:* Users can purchase one-off credit top-ups or enable auto-refill when balances drop below a designated threshold.

### Monthly Credit Mathematics: Worked Calculation

```
Theoretical Monthly Credit Totals (Assuming Full Daily Usage across 30 Days):

1. Free Tier:
   1,500 credits/day × 30 days = 45,000 maximum monthly credits ($0).

2. Pro Tier:
   (1,500 credits/day × 30 days) + 25,000 monthly bonus = 70,000 total credits ($20/mo).

3. Max Tier:
   (1,500 credits/day × 30 days) + 150,000 monthly bonus = 195,000 total credits ($100/mo).
```

### Key Pricing Insights & Gotchas:
- **Daily credits do not roll over:** The 1,500 daily credits expire every 24 hours. The theoretical "45,000 credits/month" on the Free tier only materializes if you log in and utilize credits every single day.
- **Why upgrade to Pro?** The Free tier provides roughly **64% of Pro’s total theoretical credit volume**. Therefore, upgrading to Pro ($20/mo) is only necessary if you need to run **large, credit-heavy tasks at once** (such as compiling an entire mobile app or generating 4K videos) that exceed the 1,500 daily cap.
- **Unverified Tiers:** Certain coupon aggregators mention a "$5/month Basic tier with 4,000 credits"; however, this plan is unconfirmed on official pricing pages.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is Runable and what is it used for?
Runable is an autonomous AI agent designed to create complete digital deliverables—including websites with payments, mobile apps with exportable code, slide decks, research reports, videos, and automated marketing campaigns—from plain English prompts.

### 2. Does Runable have a free plan?
Yes. Runable offers a free plan that grants **1,500 credits per day** with no upfront credit card required. However, unused daily credits do not roll over to subsequent days.

### 3. How much is Runable Pro, and how does it differ from Free?
Runable Pro costs **$20/month** (or $180/year billed annually). In addition to the 1,500 daily credits, Pro adds a **25,000 monthly credit pool** that allows users to execute larger, more compute-heavy projects in a single session.

### 4. How do Runable credits work?
Credits are deducted based on task complexity, the underlying AI model selected (Claude, GPT-4o, etc.), and the volume of assets generated. Because exact credit costs per deliverable are not publicly indexed, users must monitor their credit meter during early projects.

### 5. Can Runable build websites and mobile apps without coding?
Yes. Runable builds functional websites hosted on custom domains with built-in Stripe payments and databases. For mobile, it compiles cross-platform React Native projects for iOS and Android that can be previewed or exported.

### 6. How does Runable differ from Manus and Genspark?
While Manus focuses on complex autonomous programming for engineers and Genspark specializes in research and content summaries, Runable is built specifically for small business operators, combining digital asset creation with marketing execution (Grow ads and outreach).

### 7. Is Runable safe, and does it use my data to train AI?
Runable states in its privacy terms that it does not use customer content to train AI models. However, it lacks verified SOC 2 Type 2 certifications, hosts data on US servers (GCP Oregon), and provides limited enterprise compliance guarantees.

### 8. Can Runable use Claude, GPT, or Gemini models?
Yes. Runable features a unified multi-model selector allowing users to switch between Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Grok, DeepSeek, and Kimi within the same workspace.

### 9. Can I cancel my subscription or receive a refund?
You can cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future billing cycles. However, under Runable’s terms of service, **all subscription fees and credit purchases are strictly non-refundable**.

### 10. Who is Runable best suited for?
Runable is best suited for solo founders, independent consultants, digital marketers, and small teams that want to produce client deliverables and launch marketing initiatives without managing multiple separate SaaS subscriptions.
