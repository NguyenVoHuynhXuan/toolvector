## 5 Things to Know Before Buying

1. **Credits, not messages, are the currency.** Plans are built on Basic, Advanced and Elite credits. Which model you pick decides how fast you spend them.
2. **"Unlimited" has limits.** Reviewers point out that the top plans advertise unlimited Advanced credits but describe a threshold, after which model quality may drop. One reviewer called it ["fake-unlimited"](https://aidetectplus.com/blog/sider-ai-essay-writer-review).
3. **Prices are inconsistent across the web.** Third-party sites list Basic anywhere from about $4.20 to $10 per month depending on billing period and date. Always check Sider's official pricing at checkout.
4. **Your content goes to model providers.** Sider says it does not train models on your content, but it forwards prompts to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek or Moonshot AI depending on the model you pick. DeepSeek and Moonshot are based in China, per the privacy policy.
5. **You are buying through a reseller.** Sider decides model versions, limits and context windows. That is convenient, but you lose some of the control a direct subscription gives you.

## What Is Sider AI?

Sider is an AI assistant that started in 2023 as a Chrome sidebar called ChatGPT Sidebar and has grown into a multi-product platform. Today it spans browser extensions (Chrome, Edge, Safari), desktop apps (macOS, Windows), and iOS and Android apps. Its official pricing page claims [10M+ active users and 100K+ five-star ratings](https://sider.ai/pricing), and Chrome named it to its Editors' Picks list for 2026. Those are the company's own figures, so treat them as marketing claims.

The core idea is simple: instead of paying separately for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others, you get a single panel that sits beside whatever page you are reading. You can highlight text to translate or explain it, chat with a PDF, summarize a YouTube video, or ask several models the same question at once.

Since Sider 5.0 (March 2025), the product has moved beyond a sidebar toward research tooling: a [Deep Research agent](https://sider.ai/whats-new/browser-extension/Sider-5_0) and Wisebase, a personal knowledge base. The company behind it is listed in its privacy policy as Vidline Inc., based in Boston.

**Good fit:** students, analysts, writers, researchers, multilingual readers.  
**Poor fit:** enterprises needing audited compliance, users who prefer a single vendor's native app.

### 1. Multi-Model Chat and Group Chat

**What it does.** Sider lets you chat with models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Zhipu and Qwen. The Google Play listing lists current names such as [Grok 4.6, DeepSeek V4 variants, Kimi K3 and GLM-5](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.sider.ChatGPT.android&hl=en), plus the Sider Fusion model, which picks a model for each request.

**Real-world example.** You are drafting a product description. You send one prompt to three models at once, compare the outputs in a single thread, and keep the best sentences from each. Doing the same across three separate subscriptions means three tabs and three copy-pastes.

**Strengths.** Group chat is the feature reviewers mention most. One independent reviewer called it [genuinely unique](https://www.roborhythms.com/sider-ai-review-2026/), and another review says ["Sider's differentiator is model access"](https://futurepicker.com/?p=3747) in a four-way comparison.

**Limits.** Premium models burn Advanced credits faster, and you cannot see why a model version changed.

**Who benefits most.** People who compare models regularly: writers, marketers, developers, students.

**How to get the most from it.** Use light models for drafts, translations and quick lookups so your Advanced credits stay available for the work that really needs a frontier model. Run group chat only when the answer matters, such as a pitch, a tricky explanation or a piece of code, because every extra model in the thread spends credits too. Keep a short note of which model won for which task; after a few weeks you will know where to send each job without comparing at all. If you are unsure, the Fusion model is meant to choose for you, which is a reasonable default for casual questions.

### 2. The Page-Aware Sidebar

**What it does.** The sidebar reads the page you are on. Highlight a paragraph, press the shortcut, and ask for a summary, translation or explanation. It also handles YouTube summaries, PDFs and images. A third-party review describes [translation across 50+ languages](https://www.ai-toolbox.co/chatgpt-toolbox-competitors/sider-ai-review-2026) as a core strength.

**Real-world example.** You open a 40-page report in the browser. Instead of copying chunks into a chatbot, you ask the sidebar for a one-page summary, then follow up on a table you do not understand.

**Strengths.** Fast, ambient, and no context-switching. The difference from a chatbot site is that you stay inside the page.

**Limits.** The sidebar can feel crowded now that it carries many tools, and the Google Play reviewer feedback notes there are so many features they ["almost need organizing by category"](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.sider.ChatGPT.android&hl=en).

**Who benefits most.** Readers, researchers, non-native English speakers.

**How to get the most from it.** Learn the highlight-and-shortcut flow first; it is the fastest path from confusion to explanation. Ask for structured output, for example a three-bullet summary followed by the questions the article leaves open, rather than a generic "summarize this." For foreign-language pages, translate first and then ask follow-up questions in your own language. When a page is long or paywalled, paste the relevant section instead of relying on the whole page, since the model can only work with what it can actually read.

### 3. Deep Research and Scholar Research

**What it does.** Launched with Sider 5.0, Deep Research works through [100+ web sources](https://sider.ai/whats-new/browser-extension/Sider-5_0), reflects on what it finds and produces a cited, interactive report. Scholar Research applies the same pipeline to academic sources. According to a product listing, these run on [Elite credits](https://60minuteapps.com/tool/sider-ai/) alongside Web Creator and AI Slides.

**Real-world example.** You need a briefing on a new regulation. You type a question, wait a few minutes, then ask follow-ups to narrow the angle. The report lands in Wisebase for later.

**Strengths.** Cited output and the save-to-Wisebase step turn a one-off answer into something reusable. A review site notes that [one free Deep Research use is included](https://makerstack.co/reviews/sider-review/) for trial.

**Limits.** Like all research agents, it can miss sources or summarize them too confidently. Always open the citations on anything important. Elite credits are the scarcest on the entry plan.

**Who benefits most.** Students, analysts, consultants, journalists doing background research.

**How to get the most from it.** Spend your effort on the question, not the settings. A narrow, well-framed question (scope, time period, audience) produces a better report than a broad topic. Skim the source list before you read the summary, and drop the report into Wisebase only if the sources look credible. For anything you will publish or present, open at least the key citations yourself. Treat the report as a strong first draft of your research, not as the finished work.

### 4. Wisebase: The Personal Knowledge Base

**What it does.** Wisebase stores reports, highlights, notes, uploaded files and chats in one searchable hub. The [what's new list](https://sider.ai/whats-new/website) mentions AI file management that sorts Inbox files into topics, multi-file chat across a topic, image uploads as file content, and Wisebase Sharing.

**Real-world example.** You research three suppliers across two weeks. Each session saves findings into one topic, and at the end you ask a single question across all saved files.

**Strengths.** It closes the gap between "AI answer" and "my notes." Product Hunt reviewers cite [stronger Wisebase organization](https://www.producthunt.com/products/chatgpt-sidebar-chrome-extension) as a reason they chose Sider over Monica and Merlin.

**Limits.** One 2026 review concluded that if you want a proper knowledge base with deep organization, [Sider may not be the right tool](https://www.roborhythms.com/sider-ai-review-2026/). Dedicated note apps still handle large libraries better.

**Who benefits most.** Anyone doing multi-session research.

**How to get the most from it.** Create one topic per project from day one and send everything there: highlights, chats, reports and PDFs. Let the AI file sorting suggest topics, then rename them so you can find them later. Ask cross-file questions at milestones, for example "what do my sources disagree on?", because that is where a knowledge base beats scattered chat history. If your library grows past a few hundred items, test search quality early and keep an export habit so you are never locked in.

### 5. Hand: Browser Task Automation

**What it does.** The pricing page lists Hand as a way to automate browser tasks: [compare prices while shopping, fill out forms, research across websites and triage a Gmail inbox](https://sider.ai/pricing). The page also says higher plans bring a stronger cloud computer.

**Real-world example.** You ask Hand to compare three flight options across tabs and summarize the trade-offs instead of clicking through each site yourself.

**Strengths.** It extends Sider from "answers" to "actions," which is where most AI tools are heading.

**Limits.** Agentic features are newer and less proven than chat. We found few independent tests of Hand's reliability, so keep a human in the loop for forms, payments and email actions. This is the area where we most want readers to test before relying on it.

**Who benefits most.** Power users who already trust AI for routine web chores.

**How to get the most from it.** Start with read-only tasks such as comparing prices or gathering information across tabs, where a mistake costs you nothing. Move to form filling only after you have watched it handle a few low-stakes pages correctly. Never let an agent complete a payment, send an email or delete anything without reviewing the final step yourself. Because this area is newer, check the changelog from time to time; behavior can change faster than it does in plain chat.

### 6. Create: Images, Video, Slides and Web Pages

**What it does.** Per the pricing page, Create generates [images and videos with GPT Image 2 High, Nano Banana Pro and Seedance 2.5, plus slides and documents](https://sider.ai/pricing). Separate tools include AI Slides, Web Creator, image editing, background removal, inpainting and upscaling.

**Real-world example.** A freelancer builds a pitch deck outline, generates cover art and exports slides without leaving one account.

**Strengths.** Commercial use is listed as allowed. Breadth is high for a plan that starts under the price of a single standalone image tool.

**Limits.** Specialist tools often go deeper on a single task. Generation quality varies by model and prompt.

**Who benefits most.** Solo creators and small teams who want good-enough output fast.

**How to get the most from it.** Use Create for first drafts and variations, then finish in a specialist tool if the work is client-facing. Write prompts that name the style, audience and format, and generate a few variations before you refine one. Check the license wording for your specific use before publishing commercially, even though commercial use is listed as allowed. Slides and web pages are best treated as scaffolding you then edit, which still saves most of the blank-page time.

### 7. REC Note, Audio and ChatPDF

**What it does.** Sider supports [audio uploads with transcription and key-point extraction](https://sider.ai/whats-new/website), and REC Note records and summarizes meetings and videos. ChatPDF lets you ask questions of documents.

**Real-world example.** You record a lecture, get a transcript and then turn it into flashcards or study notes in Wisebase.

**Strengths.** The workflow ends in your knowledge base, not in a lost transcript file.

**Limits.** Meeting-first tools such as dedicated recorders may handle speaker labels and integrations better. Test accuracy on your own audio.

**Who benefits most.** Students and anyone who sits through long meetings or lectures.

**How to get the most from it.** Record in a quiet space and test one short clip before committing to a two-hour session. Ask for action items and open questions rather than a full recap, since those are what you will actually use. Send the summary to Wisebase so it connects with your other material. For legal, medical or confidential meetings, get consent from everyone present and remember that audio content is sent to the model provider you choose.

## Security & Privacy

We only list what is stated in the official [privacy policy](https://sider.ai/policies/privacy) (last updated October 19, 2026).

### What is good

- **No training on your content.** Sider states it does not use inputs, uploaded files or chat history to train or fine-tune AI models, or for research and marketing.
- **GDPR framework.** The policy lists GDPR legal bases, transfer mechanisms (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses) and rights for EEA, UK and Swiss users, including a 72-hour breach notification commitment to authorities where required.
- **Deletion is self-serve.** You can delete your account and data yourself, and account data is erased within 30 days of closure. Backups are overwritten within 90 days.
- **Retention is defined.** Usage and analytics data is kept 3 months, then anonymized or deleted.
- **Payments.** Stripe or Antom process cards; Sider says it does not store full card numbers.

### What to watch

- **Third-party model providers.** Your content is sent to the provider of the model you choose. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI are US-based; DeepSeek and Moonshot AI are in China. If your data is sensitive, avoid those models.
- **Advertising and affiliate cookies.** The policy says it uses them, and under US state law this may count as sharing for targeted ads. Opt-out tools are described in the policy.
- **Limited staff review.** Flagged content may be reviewed by staff to detect abuse.
- **No certifications found.** We did not find SOC 2, ISO 27001 or SSO documentation. The policy describes "reasonable" safeguards such as encryption in transit and access controls, and admits no system is fully secure.

**Verdict:** Fine for personal and freelance use if you avoid pasting confidential material. Regulated teams should ask Sider directly about enterprise terms before adopting it. This is not legal advice, so read the policy yourself before signing up.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Sider AI worth it?
Yes, if you regularly switch between AI models while browsing and want research and note tools included. If you use one model only, a direct subscription is simpler.
