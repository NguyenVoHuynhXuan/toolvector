### Definitive Pricing & Subscription Breakdown

Higgsfield provides Individual plans for creators and Business plans for larger teams. The Individual plans, billed annually, include:

- **Starter Plan ($15/mo)**: Designed for first-time AI creators. Includes 200 credits/mo (approx. 100 Nano Banana Pro or 11 Seedance 2.0 Fast generations). Includes 3 unlimited & free generation models, but no access to Seedance 2.5 or 2.0, and no unlimited generations for premium models.
- **Plus Plan ($39/mo)**: 20% off for everyday AI creation. Includes 1,000 credits/mo. Unlocks full access to Seedance 2.5 (1080p) and Seedance 2.0 (4K). Offers 7-day unlimited generations for Nano Banana 2 (2K) and Kling 3.0, plus 7 unlimited & free generation models.
- **Ultra Plan ($99/mo)**: 23% off (Best Value) for ambitious projects. Base tier starts at 3,000 credits/mo (scalable up to 9,000). Unlocks 7-day unlimited generations for the flagship Nano Banana Pro (2K) alongside Nano Banana 2 and Kling 3.0. Full access to Seedance models included.

*Billing & Refund Note*: Payments are processed securely via Stripe (Credit/Debit, Apple Pay)[cite: 1]. Subscriptions renew automatically[cite: 1]. A 7-day refund window is available for initial purchases provided no credits have been consumed[cite: 1]. Individual credits do not roll over between billing cycles[cite: 1].
