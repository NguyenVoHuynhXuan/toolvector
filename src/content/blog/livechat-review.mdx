## 5 Things to Know Before Buying Text (LiveChat)

1. **The brand has unified under “Text”:** Text S.A. is actively converging LiveChat, ChatBot, and HelpDesk into a unified customer platform. While standalone products remain accessible, accounts are increasingly managed under the Text suite umbrella.
2. **There is no perpetual free plan:** Text offers a full-featured **14-day free trial** with no credit card required, but once it expires, you must subscribe to a paid tier. Freelancers needing a $0 chat tool should look at Tidio or Crisp instead.
3. **Billing is strictly per user seat:** Every agent account created in your dashboard is billed, even if that team member rarely logs in. Team managers must remember to deactivate dormant seats promptly to avoid unnecessary renewal charges.
4. **The AI Agent operates on monthly resolution quotas:** Each plan includes a set allowance of automated AI resolutions (e.g., 10 on Essential, 200 on Growth). Any usage beyond that automatically triggers 50-resolution packs at $49.50 (~$0.99 per resolution).
5. **Built-in ticketing is functional, not enterprise-grade:** While incoming requests can be converted into tickets with chat transcripts attached, Capterra reviewers consistently rate the ticketing module as “adequate” rather than a replacement for Zendesk or Freshdesk.

## What Is Text (LiveChat)?

Text (originally launched as LiveChat) was founded in 2002 in Wrocław, Poland by **Text S.A.** (WSE: TXT). Over two decades, it has grown into one of the most widely deployed customer communication platforms in the world, serving over **30,000 businesses across 150 countries**.

While historically known as an on-site chat widget, Text has evolved into an **omnichannel conversational commerce platform**. It combines:
- A responsive chat widget with file sharing, canned responses, and sneak-peek typing.
- An autonomous **24/7 AI Agent** capable of learning from help documentation and answering store inquiries.
- A **Customer Data Platform (CDP)** that aggregates browsing history, shopping carts, and custom user attributes.
- Native e-commerce integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce to trigger automated abandoned-cart recovery.

## Key Features: Deep Dive

### 1. Real-Time Visitor Tracking & Customer Profiles (CDP)

One of Text’s distinct competitive advantages over lightweight chat widgets is its real-time visitor radar. Rather than simply waiting for a customer to click “Chat,” agents can see a live feed of active website visitors.

The console reveals:
- The exact URL the visitor is viewing and how many minutes they have been on the page.
- Returning visitor indicators and previous conversation history.
- Real-time shopping cart contents, item counts, and live basket totals (via Shopify/WooCommerce).
- Consolidated customer profile attributes (email, location, company name, custom tags).

> **Practical Example:** An agent notices a customer from Germany dwelling on the `/checkout` page for 3 minutes with a $513 cart. The agent can proactively reach out with: *“Hi Anna! I see you’re at checkout—can I confirm our shipping timelines or help with a payment question?”*

*Plan Limitation:* Concurrent tracked visitors are capped based on your subscription tier (50 concurrent visitors on Essential, 400 on Growth). Stores with massive concurrent flash-sale traffic will need higher tiers.

### Understanding AI Resolution Overages

When your AI Agent answers more customer inquiries than your monthly plan allowance provides:
- Overages are billed automatically in **packs of 50 resolutions for $49.50** (equivalent to **$0.99 per resolution**).
- A resolution is defined as a chat where the AI successfully resolves the user's inquiry without escalating to a human agent. Multiple questions within the same chat session count as a single resolution.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Is Text (LiveChat) worth it in 2026?
Yes, particularly for online retailers and subscription SaaS businesses that generate revenue through chat interactions. The combination of live visitor cart monitoring and revenue attribution gives it a demonstrable return on investment that simple chat widgets cannot match.

### 2. Is there a free plan available?
No. Text does not provide a permanent free plan. However, they offer a **14-day free trial** that includes full access to core features without requiring credit card details.

### 3. How much does Text realistically cost per month?
For a single operator, expect roughly $19 to $25 per month on annual billing, plus any AI resolution overages. For a 3-agent team on the Growth plan with moderate AI usage, typical monthly budgets range between $237 and $400.

### 4. How does an “AI resolution” count toward your bill?
An AI resolution is counted when the AI Agent provides a complete answer that resolves the customer's question without escalating to a human agent. Multiple back-and-forth messages within the same conversation count as only one resolution.

### 5. Does Text use my customer chat data to train AI models?
No. Text’s privacy policy specifies that your company’s customer conversation transcripts and private help desk data are isolated and are **not used to train global public AI models**.

### 6. What happens if I exceed my AI resolution allowance?
Your AI Agent will not abruptly shut down. Instead, an additional pack of 50 resolutions is automatically provisioned for $49.50 (~$0.99 per resolution) to ensure uninterrupted customer service.

### 7. What are Text’s cancellation and refund policies?
Subscriptions can be canceled at any time from your account management panel, remaining active until the end of the current paid billing cycle. According to Text’s standard terms of service, payments are non-refundable except where required by local consumer law.

### 8. Does Text support WhatsApp and Instagram messaging?
Yes. WhatsApp Business, Instagram Direct, and Facebook Messenger conversations can be routed directly into the Text agent dashboard via marketplace integrations.

### 9. How does LiveChat compare with Tidio and Zendesk?
Text offers deeper real-time visitor tracking and e-commerce cart monitoring than Tidio, and is much faster and cheaper to deploy than Zendesk. However, Zendesk remains superior for complex enterprise ticketing, while Tidio offers a free entry plan for micro-businesses.
