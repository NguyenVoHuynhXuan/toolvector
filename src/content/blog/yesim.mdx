## 1. What is Yesim?

Yesim is a Swiss-based travel eSIM provider launched in 2019, focused on delivering seamless mobile data connectivity for travelers moving between multiple countries. Instead of swapping physical SIM cards every time you cross a border, Yesim lets you install a single eSIM and switch plans directly in the app whenever you arrive at a new destination.

<Image src={yesimPic1} alt="Yesim app interface" class="prose-img" />

What makes Yesim stand out among the many eSIM apps on the market today is that it doesn't just sell plain mobile data. Yesim bundles a free VPN and a virtual phone number service into the same app — a combination that very few direct competitors offer.

## 2. How It Works

Using Yesim is fairly similar to other eSIM providers on the market, but the process is streamlined to the essentials:

1. Download the Yesim app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
2. Create an account using your email or a quick Apple/Google sign-in.
3. Choose your destination (by country, region, or a global plan).
4. Select a plan type: fixed data, unlimited, or Pay & Fly.
5. Pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay, or a credit card.
6. Install the eSIM directly in the app (scan the QR code or install directly).
7. Activate it upon arrival and enable data roaming.

<Image src={yesimPic2} alt="Yesim installation steps" class="prose-img" />

The entire process typically takes under 10 minutes, and users can schedule activation up to 30 days in advance — handy for preparing ahead of a trip.

## 3. Plan Types

Yesim offers a fairly diverse plan catalog to suit different types of travelers:

| Plan Type | Details | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed Data Plans** | From a few GB up to 20GB, valid 7-30 days | Short trips with predictable data needs |
| **Unlimited Plans** | High speed up to a certain threshold (~50GB), then throttled | Users who want peace of mind on data |
| **Pay & Fly** | Pay only for the data you actually use, no fixed expiration | Travelers with unpredictable itineraries |
| **Unlim Day Pass** | Buy unlimited-data days (10, 20, 80 days...) | Frequent travelers / business trips |

A notable plus: even Yesim's unlimited plans support Hotspot sharing — a feature not every eSIM provider allows.

## 4. Coverage

Yesim partners with more than 800 mobile operators across over 200 countries and territories. With its Global eSIM plan, a single eSIM can cover 140+ countries, so you don't need to buy a separate eSIM for each leg of your trip — useful for a multi-country tour across Europe or Southeast Asia, for example.

## 5. Free VPN — The Standout Feature

This is arguably the feature that makes Yesim stand out from most of the travel eSIM market. Rather than just selling data, Yesim bundles a VPN directly into the app, activated with a single toggle.

This VPN helps encrypt your traffic when using public Wi-Fi at airports, cafés, or hotels — a genuinely practical security need while traveling. That said, there are a few limitations worth noting:

- **iOS only** — Android users are not currently supported for this feature.
- **No independently audited no-logs policy** — Yesim states it doesn't log traffic, but this hasn't been verified by a third-party auditor.

In terms of cost, a standalone VPN like NordVPN runs about $3-4/month on a long-term plan — so getting a free VPN bundled with Yesim is still a meaningful advantage, especially for iPhone users.

## 6. Virtual Phone Numbers

Beyond data and VPN, Yesim also sells virtual phone numbers for select countries including the US, Canada, UK, Netherlands, Israel, and Ukraine, starting at roughly $3/month. This is particularly useful when you need to receive SMS verification codes (OTPs) from banking apps, social media, or other services while abroad, without using your primary phone number.

Note that Yesim's virtual numbers only support SMS — they do not support traditional two-way voice calls like a regular physical SIM.

## 7. Is the Pricing Worth It?

Yesim plans start at around €0.53/day for smaller plans, which is competitive with the broader eSIM market. That said, a few things are worth considering:

- If you only need a small data plan (1-2GB) for a short single-country trip, competitors like Airalo may be cheaper since they offer more small-size options.
- If you need truly unlimited data for heavy video streaming, Holafly offers unlimited plans with no speed throttling, though usually at a higher price.
- Yesim is the best fit for travelers who want the **combination**: data + VPN + virtual number in a single app, especially those crossing multiple countries frequently.

## 12. Final Verdict

Yesim is a solid eSIM option worth considering, especially for travelers who move between multiple countries frequently and want an all-in-one solution: mobile data, VPN, and virtual phone numbers, packaged into a single app. Its accessible starting price and quick activation process make it a good fit for both casual travelers and frequent business travelers alike.

That said, if you only need a small data plan for a short single-country trip, or require truly unlimited data for constant streaming, competitors like Airalo or Holafly may be a better fit. For most travelers who value convenience, flexibility, and an extra layer of VPN security, Yesim is still a name worth trying.
