## 5 Things to Know Before Buying Pixelcut

1. **Plan names and pricing tiers have shifted repeatedly:** Pro currently costs **$10/month** (or **$8/month billed annually**), while Business is **$30/month** (or **$24/month billed annually**). The current Business tier (offering 3,600 credits) was marketed as “Pro+” in late 2025 before being rebranded to Business by mid-2026. Be cautious of older third-party guides referencing outdated “Max” or “Pro+” plans.
2. **Credits only apply to generative AI models:** Paying subscribers enjoy practically unlimited use of foundational utility tools—including background removal, Magic Eraser, and Fast Upscaler. Credits are only deducted when invoking compute-heavy generative models like AI Product Photos, AI UGC Ads, and custom Creative Agent workflows.
3. **Cancellations do not yield prorated refunds:** If you cancel a paid monthly or annual subscription, your account remains active until the end of the current billing cycle, but Pixelcut does not issue partial or prorated refunds for unused time.
4. **The Free tier has undocumented daily caps:** While the official pricing page simply lists the Free tier as “Limited,” Pixelcut’s customer help center clarifies that free web usage is capped at approximately **3 operations per day**.
5. **No confirmed enterprise security standards:** We found no public verification of SAML/SSO authentication, SOC 2 Type 2 reports, or ISO 27001 compliance. Businesses with strict IT compliance mandates should engage Pixelcut sales directly before uploading proprietary product prototypes.

## Key Features: In-Depth Analysis

### 1. Automated Background Removal (Image & Video)

Background cutout accuracy is the foundation of Pixelcut’s product suite. The tool employs edge-detection neural networks to isolate complex subjects—including transparent glassware, jewelry, fine hair, and textile fabrics.

- **Process:** Drag and drop an image or MP4 video → processing takes 1 to 3 seconds → export as a transparent PNG/ProRes video or immediately place the subject against a virtual studio backdrop.
- **Usage Limits:** The Free plan permits roughly 3 daily operations. On Pro and Business tiers, background cutouts are classified as **practically unlimited** and do not consume generative credits.
- **Comparison:** While Photoroom provides slightly deeper batch-syncing with Shopify storefronts, Pixelcut’s cutout quality is on par with the best standalone tools in the industry.
- **Feature Score:** **4.7/5**

### 2. Creative Agent (v3)

Launched as a major milestone, **Creative Agent v3** transforms visual editing into an autonomous, natural-language workflow.

### 3. Topaz AI Upscaler (Up to 16x Resolution)

In late 2025, Pixelcut integrated **Topaz Labs** neural upscaling technology directly into its platform, giving users access to desktop-grade enlargement without buying standalone desktop licenses.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How much does Pixelcut cost in 2026?
Pixelcut Pro costs **$10 per month** on monthly billing or **$8 per month** ($96/year) when billed annually, including 3 seats and 600 monthly credits. The Business plan costs **$30 per month** (or **$24/month billed annually**), including 10 seats, 3,600 credits, and 2,000 monthly batch exports.

### 2. Is Pixelcut completely free to use?
Pixelcut offers a free web tier, but it is limited to approximately **3 image operations per day** and restricts access to advanced generative models and high-resolution Topaz upscaling.

### 3. Can I use Pixelcut images for commercial purposes?
Yes. Paid subscribers hold commercial rights to the visual assets generated and edited through Pixelcut, making them suitable for Amazon, Shopify, social media advertisements, and print marketing.

### 4. Does Pixelcut train its AI models on my uploaded photos?
According to Pixelcut’s published developer documentation, customer uploads are not used to train global AI models without user consent. However, consumer privacy documentation remains brief regarding long-term storage across standard web interfaces.

### 5. How do I cancel Pixelcut, and can I get a refund?
You can cancel your subscription through your web account settings or your Apple App Store / Google Play subscription menu. Canceling stops future renewals, but your plan remains active until the end of the paid period. Pixelcut does not offer prorated refunds for remaining days.

### 6. Pixelcut vs. Photoroom: Which is better for e-commerce sellers?
Pixelcut offers better team value (3 to 10 seats included) and unique features like Claude MCP and AI video ads. Photoroom is slightly faster for high-volume automated Shopify catalog syncing.

### 7. What are Pixelcut credits, and do basic cutouts use them?
Credits are consumed only when invoking compute-intensive generative tools (AI Product Photos, Generative Fill, and AI UGC Ads). Core utilities like background removal, Magic Eraser, and Fast Upscaler do not consume credits on paid tiers.

### 8. Does Pixelcut provide an API?
Yes. Pixelcut provides an official REST API that allows developers to integrate background removal, upscaling, and generative photo tools directly into custom e-commerce applications.

### 9. How does Pixelcut work with Claude?
Pixelcut provides an official Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. Once configured in Claude Desktop, users can instruct Claude to remove backgrounds or upscale photos using their existing Pixelcut credits directly within chat.

### 10. Was Pixelcut renamed Pixa?
Yes. The platform briefly adopted the name **Pixa** in early 2026 before reverting back to its established **Pixelcut** brand name.
