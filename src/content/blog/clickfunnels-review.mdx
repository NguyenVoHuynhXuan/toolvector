*How ClickFunnels turns cold traffic into high-margin buyers: capture leads, expand cart value with Order Bumps and 1-Click OTO Upsells, and trigger instant course access + CRM follow-up.*

What separates ClickFunnels from generic website builders like WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace is that every element is engineered around **Direct-Response Unit Economics (CPA vs. AOV)**:
- **2-Step Smart Checkout (Abandoned Cart Recovery):** Step 1 of the order form captures the buyer’s Name, Email, and Phone Number *before* displaying credit card fields on Step 2. If they accept, their card is charged in one click; if they decline, ClickFunnels automatically routes them to a lower-priced **Downsell (OTO 2)** or split-pay plan.
