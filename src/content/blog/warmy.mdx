### What Is Warmy.io?
<Image src={warmyPic1} alt="Warmy Review" class="prose-img" />

Operated by AnyBiz LTD, Warmy.io is a specialized deliverability and email warm-up ecosystem. Rather than acting as a standard cold email sender, Warmy focuses entirely on infrastructure security and inbox placement optimization.

The platform supports all major email services—including Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Zoho, and Amazon SES—making it a versatile tool for cold email marketers, SDR teams, SaaS startups, lead generation agencies, and companies launching brand-new domains.

### How Does Warmy Work?

Warmy automates the gradual warming process of an email address to build trust with internet service providers (ISPs). Its automated network sends and receives messages, generates multi-turn reply threads, rescues emails from spam folders, marks messages as important, and archives processed threads.

- **Connect**: Link your mailbox (Gmail, Outlook, custom SMTP/IMAP).
- **Configure**: Set your daily sending speed, language preferences, and warm-up topics.
- **Automate**: Let Warmy's network generate organic engagement signals.
- **Monitor**: Track real-time placement and DNS health metrics via the central dashboard.

### Core Features & Real Benefits Exploded

<Image src={warmyPic2} alt="Warmy Core Features" class="prose-img" />

- **Automated Warm-Up Engine**: Generates organic, multi-turn email conversations that open messages, reply in threads, mark emails as priority, and automatically pull messages out of spam folders.
- **Inbox Deliverability & Seed List Testing**: Run pre-campaign placement tests against live seed lists across major email providers to detect spam triggers before sending to real prospects.
- **Infrastructure & DNS Monitoring**: Tracks critical authentication records in real time, including SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX, and rDNS setups, identifying technical flaws that harm inbox placement.
- **Google Postmaster Integration**: Direct integration provides granular visibility into domain reputation, spam rate flags, and delivery errors across Google's infrastructure.
- **Template Warm-Up & Testing**: Warm up specific sales templates alongside your mailboxes to evaluate whether message content or copy triggers ISP spam filters.
- **Adeline AI Optimization**: An integrated AI assistant that analyzes mailbox metrics, deliverability trends, and warm-up speed to provide tailored troubleshooting recommendations.
- **Agency Workspaces**: Designed for agencies and large outbound teams to manage multiple client mailboxes under dedicated workspaces with custom permissions.
