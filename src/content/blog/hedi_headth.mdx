## 1. What is Heidi Health?

Heidi Health is an ambient AI medical scribe founded in 2019 in Melbourne, Australia. Instead of typing notes during or after a consultation, clinicians let Heidi listen to the visit (in person or over telehealth) and generate structured documentation automatically — SOAP notes, referral letters, and summaries — in the clinician's own template and style.

<Image src={heidiPic2} alt="Heidi Health app interface generating a clinical note" class="prose-img" />

The platform has grown quickly outside Australia too, particularly in the UK, Europe, and increasingly the US, and now says it supports over 2.4 million consultations every week. In February 2026, Heidi restructured its plans and folded in a new feature called Heidi Evidence, a citation-backed clinical reference tool built directly into the sidebar.

## 2. How It Works

Getting started with Heidi is designed to take only a few minutes:

1. Sign up for a free account on the web app or mobile app (iOS/Android) — no credit card required.
2. Choose your specialty and pick or customize a note template (SOAP, referral, progress note, etc.).
3. Start a session before or at the beginning of the consultation, in person or via telehealth.
4. Heidi listens in the background and transcribes the conversation in real time.
5. At the end of the visit, Heidi generates a structured note in your chosen template.
6. Review, edit, and use Ask Heidi to refine specific sections with natural-language commands.
7. Copy the note into your EHR manually, or push it directly if you're on a plan with write-back access.

Multiple people can also contribute to the same encounter note — useful in clinics where a nurse or medical assistant handles intake and a physician finishes the visit, with timestamps tracking who added what.

## 3. Plan Types

Heidi's February 2026 rebrand introduced new plan names and folded Heidi Evidence into every tier:

| Plan | Price | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | $0 | Unlimited transcription, unlimited standard-template notes, unlimited Evidence lookups outside sessions, 10 Pro Actions/month |
| **Clinician** | $150/month (billed annually) | Solo clinicians who want unlimited Ask Heidi, premium templates, and full Evidence access in-session |
| **Practice** | Custom, contact sales | Clinics that need EHR write-back, collaborative documentation, and admin controls |
| **Enterprise** | Custom, contact sales | Health systems needing deep EHR integration (e.g. native Epic workspace), SSO, and dedicated support |

A distinguishing point in Heidi's pricing philosophy: core functionality — transcription, standard-template notes, and unlimited Evidence outside of sessions — stays free indefinitely, rather than being a time-limited trial.

## 4. Language and Specialty Coverage

Heidi supports more than 110 languages for transcription and note generation, which is broader than most competitors in the AI scribe space and a real advantage for practices serving multilingual patient populations rather than a marketing checkbox.

On the specialty side, Heidi covers family medicine, internal medicine, psychiatry, pediatrics, OB/GYN, orthopedics, dermatology, and more, with templates that can be customized per specialty and per clinician.

## 5. Heidi Evidence — The 2026 Addition

Alongside the pricing rebrand, Heidi launched Evidence: a clinical reference tool that lets clinicians ask a clinical question and get a citation-backed answer grounded in guidelines, medical references, and primary research, with every key statement linked back to its source.

A few practical notes on how it's gated:

- **Outside of sessions**, Evidence is unlimited on every plan, including Free.
- **Inside a live consult** (via Ask Heidi), Evidence is unlimited only on paid plans (Clinician and above).
- **EU and UK users** currently only get Evidence outside of sessions, regardless of plan.

## 6. EHR Integration

Heidi offers native integrations with Epic (launching directly inside the Epic workspace), athenahealth, Best Practice, Cliniko, and MediRecords. This is one of the more consequential details to check before subscribing: EHR write-back — automatically pushing a finished note into the patient's chart — is not included on the self-serve Clinician plan. It requires a Practice or Enterprise contract, which is priced individually rather than published on the pricing page.

For a solo clinician on Clinician at $150/month, this means notes still need to be copied into the EHR manually, even though the platform physically connects to it.

## 7. Is the Pricing Worth It?

Heidi's pricing story has shifted meaningfully. Before 2026, the paid tier sat around $90/month billed annually; it's now $150/month on the same billing terms, with monthly billing priced higher still. A few things worth weighing:

- If your documentation needs are light and standard-template notes are enough, the **Free plan** may cover you indefinitely — unlike most competitors' free tiers, it isn't a time-limited trial.
- If you need EHR write-back without moving to a custom Practice/Enterprise contract, Heidi's self-serve tier won't get you there — some competitors bundle this at self-serve pricing.
- If multilingual support and template flexibility matter more than price, Heidi's strengths in those areas are hard to match at any tier.

## 8. Security and Compliance

Heidi is HIPAA compliant and provides a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for US-based practices, alongside SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, PIPEDA (Canada), APP (Australia), and NHS compliance for UK practices. Data is localized in the US, UK, EU, Canada, and Australia depending on where the practice operates. As with any AI documentation tool, the practice itself remains responsible for access controls and staff training around protected health information.

## 12. Final Verdict

Heidi Health remains a legitimate, well-built option in the AI medical scribe space, with genuine strengths in language coverage and template customization that few competitors match. Its free plan is also unusually generous for the category, making it easy to test in real clinical use before paying anything.

That said, 2026 pricing puts the Clinician tier at a premium compared to some newer entrants, and the fact that EHR write-back sits behind a custom Practice or Enterprise contract is worth confirming before you commit, especially if direct chart integration is the main reason you're evaluating an AI scribe. For multilingual outpatient practices that value flexibility over the lowest possible price, Heidi is still very much worth trying.
