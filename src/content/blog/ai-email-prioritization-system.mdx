## How PriorityEngine Works: 5-Stage Pipeline & 7 Signal Dimensions

<Image src={pic1} alt="PriorityEngine Business Signal Intelligence overview transforming incoming customer, financial, opportunity, and follow-up emails into resolved business signals" class="prose-img" />

*PriorityEngine’s core architecture: evaluating incoming communications across 7 signal nodes (Intent, Identity, Context, Facts, Impact, Attention, Priority) to surface high-impact actions.*

Instead of sorting mail by sender address, subject keywords, or arrival timestamp, PriorityEngine processes connected business communications through a structured three-layer philosophy:
- **Business Information:** `Messages · Requests · Documents`
- **AI Intelligence:** `Meaning · Context · Impact`
- **Business Clarity:** `Priorities · Insights · Next Steps`

### 1. The 7 Signal Evaluation Nodes

Every analyzed email is evaluated as a piece of business communication across seven operational dimensions:
1. **Intent (*Understand the goal*):** What is the sender actually asking for, proposing, or reporting?
2. **Identity (*Who is involved*):** Is this an active client, prospective enterprise buyer, supplier, financial institution, or automated newsletter?
3. **Context (*Business meaning*):** How does this message relate to revenue, active deliverables, billing, compliance, or operations?
4. **Facts (*Operational signals*):** Are there concrete deadlines, invoice numbers, blocked tasks, or unanswered commitments embedded in the text?
5. **Impact (*Business effect*):** What is the commercial, financial, or reputational cost if this thread waits 48 hours?
6. **Attention (*Needs focus?*):** Does a human need to intervene right now, or can this safely wait for a batch review window?
7. **Priority (*What comes first*):** How should this item rank relative to everything else competing for your team’s focus today?

### 2. The 5-Stage Inbox Intelligence Pipeline

<Image src={pic2} alt="PriorityEngine five-stage inbox intelligence pipeline: Understand, Detect, Assess, Prioritize, and Act" class="prose-img" />

*PriorityEngine doesn't make every email urgent—it determines what deserves your attention through five sequential stages: Understand, Detect, Assess, Prioritize, and Act.*

Once an email enters PriorityEngine from Gmail, Microsoft 365, Outlook, or IMAP, it moves through five distinct stages:

1. **Understand:** Identifies the message type, sender intent, participants, and surrounding business context beyond surface subject lines.
2. **Detect:** Separates genuine business signals (**Opportunities, Customer Issues, Financial Matters, Commitments, Operational Risks**) from newsletters, promotions, and routine system noise.
3. **Assess:** Evaluates business impact (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`), attention requirements (`REQUIRED` vs. `LOW SIGNAL`), timing, and deadlines.
4. **Prioritize:** Determines what deserves visibility at the top of your workspace first—Guided by the principle that *"Important does not always mean urgent."*
5. **Act:** Translates the analysis into an explainable **"Why It Matters"** summary plus a concrete recommended action: **`Reply`**, **`Review`**, **`Investigate`**, **`Monitor`**, **`Finish`**, or **`Ignore`**.
