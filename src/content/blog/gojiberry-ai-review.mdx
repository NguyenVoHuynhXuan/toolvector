## What Is Gojiberry?

<Image src={pic1} alt="Gojiberry AI — signal-based AI GTM agent for LinkedIn and email outreach" class="prose-img" />



Gojiberry (Gojiberry AI) is a B2B prospecting and outreach platform that describes itself as an AI sales agent, or an "AI GTM team" for small outbound teams. You give it your website address. It reads the site to understand what you sell, who you target and how to pitch, then looks for people showing signs of buying interest and contacts them across LinkedIn and email.

The company is operated by Superfruits SAS in Paris, with Superfruits, Inc. in Delaware listed as the parent company in the privacy policy. Its Y Combinator profile describes a founding team with prior startup exits: one co-founder previously built the Shopify app CoCo AI, and another co-founded the attendance SaaS Edusign. A third-party revenue tracker, ARR Club, relays a founder post claiming close to $5M in annual recurring revenue about 14 months after launch. We treat that as a founder-reported figure rather than audited data.

**Gojiberry is a good fit for:**
- Founders who need pipeline without hiring an SDR yet
- Small sales teams (roughly 1 to 5 people) with a clear ICP
- Agencies running LinkedIn-led outbound
- Anyone who wants signals, enrichment and outreach in one subscription

**It is a poor fit for:**
- High-volume email blasting teams
- Companies that need SSO, formal compliance reports or procurement-heavy contracts
- Teams selling to audiences who are not active on LinkedIn
- Buyers who want guaranteed meetings or refunds

## Key Features: Deep Dive

### 1. Signal Detection and Lookalike Audiences
<Image src={pic2} alt="Gojiberry AI — signal-based AI GTM agent for LinkedIn and email outreach" class="prose-img" />

**What it does.** Gojiberry monitors signals that suggest a prospect is more likely to care. According to its FAQ, these include people engaging with your company or content, profile visits, company followers, engagement around competitors, activity on relevant topics, job changes, hiring activity and lookalikes of your best customers. The product page references 15+ buying and social signals.

**Example in practice.** A founder selling an AI meeting tool could target people who follow or react to posts from competing vendors, then layer on a job-change signal for new heads of sales. Instead of pulling a static list of "VPs of Sales at SaaS companies," the agent surfaces people who did something *recently*.

**Strengths.** Timing is the real edge. Reaching someone while a trigger is fresh is easier to justify in a message than a generic pitch.

**Limits.** Signals are only as good as the public activity available. Audiences that are quiet on LinkedIn will produce fewer leads.

**Compared with alternatives.** lemlist also sells intent signals, but as a paid add-on (100 signals per month for about $21 on yearly pricing). Gojiberry bundles signals into the Pro plan.

**Who benefits most.** Teams with a narrow ICP whose buyers are active on LinkedIn.

---

### 2. ICP Filtering and Lead Scoring
<Image src={pic4} alt="Gojiberry AI — signal-based AI GTM agent for LinkedIn and email outreach" class="prose-img" />

**What it does.** Each lead is enriched, checked against your ideal customer profile, scored and prioritized so the most promising contacts are messaged first. The company's positioning is that it avoids wasting messages on people who never fit.

**Strengths.** Pre-filtering protects your reputation and your LinkedIn account. Sending fewer, better-targeted messages is also kinder to prospects.

**Limits.** The scoring logic is not documented in detail. Review early lists manually and tighten the ICP definition when results look off.

**Who benefits most.** Anyone who has been burned by list-buying and wants to see the reasoning behind each lead.

---

### 3. Multichannel Outreach with AI-Written Messages

**What it does.** Gojiberry runs LinkedIn connection requests, LinkedIn messages and email follow-ups from one workflow, with conditional steps and automatic follow-ups. Messages are written around the prospect's company, activity, signals and timing. You do not build a classic sequence step by step.

**Strengths.** One system replaces separate scraping, writing and sending tools. The "no sequences to build" approach cuts setup time for non-specialists.

**Limits.** Less control than a sequencer built for power users. Teams that want granular A/B testing, custom deliverability tooling or phone and SMS steps will find lemlist or similar tools broader. AI-written messages still need a human eye; approval mode exists for that reason.

**Who benefits most.** Small teams that want outreach running without a dedicated ops person.

---

### 4. Approval Mode, Drafted Replies and Unified Inbox
<Image src={pic5} alt="Gojiberry AI — signal-based AI GTM agent for LinkedIn and email outreach" class="prose-img" />

**What it does.** You can run fully autonomous or approve messages before they go out. When a prospect replies, a tailored response is drafted and waiting in the inbox, ready to send in one click.

**Strengths.** This is a sensible safety valve. Start in approval mode, learn what the agent writes, then loosen control when you trust it.

**Limits.** Drafted replies are only as good as the context the agent has. Keep a human on any conversation that matters.

**Who benefits most.** Founders who want speed but not blind automation.

---

### 5. Enrichment, Website Visitor ID and Integrations

**What it does.** Pro lists email and phone waterfall enrichment across 15+ data providers, website visitor identification, and integrations with HubSpot, Pipedrive, Attio, an API and MCP. The site also has connection pages for Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Muse and Dots.

**Claude MCP example.** Gojiberry's Claude page shows prompts such as asking for 20 SaaS founders hiring SDRs in the US, then asking for a message for each, or asking which job titles replied most this week. It claims setup takes about two minutes with no code.

**Limits.** Website visitor identification is described as company-level rather than individual-level. Check consent requirements for your own visitors before using this feature.

**Who benefits most.** Teams that want leads flowing into a CRM without manual copy-paste.

---

### 6. The Weekly Learning Loop
<Image src={pic3} alt="Gojiberry AI — signal-based AI GTM agent for LinkedIn and email outreach" class="prose-img" />

**What it does.** Gojiberry says the agent tracks what converts, adjusts its approach each week and benchmarks campaigns against top performers in your industry.

**Our take.** This is the hardest claim to verify from outside. It is plausible that an agent tuning ICP and messaging weekly improves over time, but we found no independent data showing how large the improvement is. Treat it as a feature to test during your trial, not a proven advantage.

## Security and Privacy

*Based on Gojiberry's privacy policy (last updated October 13, 2026) and general terms (last updated 14/10/2025).*

### What is documented

- **Encryption:** in transit and at rest, with access controls, logging, incident-response processes and backups.
- **GDPR and UK GDPR:** legal bases (including legitimate interests for B2B contact data), data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability), and a dedicated Privacy Choices opt-out page for business contacts.
- **International transfers:** adequacy decisions, EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **California:** a separate CCPA notice exists.
- **Payments:** handled by Stripe. Gojiberry says it does not store full card details.
- **Subprocessors:** available on request rather than published.

### What we could not find

- No mention of SOC 2 or ISO 27001 reports
- No mention of SSO/SAML on the Pro plan
- No published data residency options
- No explicit statement about whether customer data is used to train AI models

### A contract detail worth reading twice

The privacy policy says Gojiberry acts as a **controller** for some activities, including enrichment of professional contact data, and as a **processor** for customer-provided data. The general terms say the customer is the **data controller** and Gojiberry acts as a **processor**. These documents were updated a year apart and do not line up neatly. If you operate in the EU or UK, ask Gojiberry for a data processing agreement and clarify roles before running large campaigns.

### LinkedIn safety

Gojiberry describes human-like sending limits, smart pacing and quality filters. LinkedIn's own rules restrict automated activity, and Gojiberry's terms explicitly disclaim liability for account restrictions. Use conservative volume, warm targeting and approval mode. Never run outreach from an account you cannot afford to lose.

**Verdict:** Fine for individuals, startups and most SMBs. If your organization requires SSO, a SOC 2 report or a signed security questionnaire, talk to sales first.
