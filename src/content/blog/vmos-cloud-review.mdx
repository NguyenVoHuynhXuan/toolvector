## 5 Crucial Things to Know Before Subscribing to VMOS Cloud

1. **You don’t need a monthly plan just to test:** VMOS Cloud offers **pay-per-minute timed devices** (such as Timed V06 at roughly **\$0.006/minute**, with daily spend caps available). Spin up a timed instance first to test your exact APK, proxy speed, and latency for under \$1 before buying monthly blocks.
2. **Proxies are billed separately—and matter enormously for social accounts:** A cloud phone isolates your **hardware fingerprint, IMEI, and Android ID**, but platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and WhatsApp also inspect your IP address. Always pair each cloud phone with a clean **Static ISP or Residential proxy** (such as [Webshare](/blog/webshare-review/) or OwlProxy) matched to your target country.
3. **Choose V04 (8GB RAM) or Flagship (12GB RAM) for heavy video & strict apps:** While **V08 (4GB RAM / \$4.99/mo)** works fine for lightweight messaging apps or 2D idle games, modern video-heavy apps like **TikTok Shop, CapCut, Instagram Reels, or 3D RPGs** run far more smoothly on **V04 (8GB RAM / \$8.99/mo)**, **V03 (10.7GB RAM / \$10.99/mo)**, or **Flagship profiles (12GB RAM / \$13.99/mo)**.
4. **Check your subscription auto-renewal settings immediately after checkout:** A recurring theme in VMOS Cloud’s public Trustpilot reviews is users forgetting that monthly cloud devices auto-renew and struggling to obtain retroactive refunds. Treat cloud phone billing like cloud VPS billing—turn off auto-renew on experimental instances immediately.
5. **Team Collaboration eliminates risky password sharing:** Agencies running client accounts can assign specific cloud phones to remote VAs inside the **Team Center** with granular permissions—letting staff operate logged-in apps remotely without ever handing over raw passwords or 2FA SIM codes.
