## 5 Crucial Things to Know Before Subscribing to StreamYard

1. **The \$14.99/mo Essential plan does NOT remove the StreamYard watermark:** Read the pricing table closely before checking out. While **Essential (\$14.99/mo annual · \$18.99/mo monthly)** unlocks Full HD 1080p and 2-channel multistreaming, **removing the StreamYard corner logo and uploading your own custom logos, overlays, and backgrounds requires the Core plan (\$35.99/mo annual · \$44.99/mo monthly)**.
2. **Cloud multistreaming uses zero extra local upload bandwidth:** Unlike OBS multistream plugins that force your home internet to upload 4 or 5 separate video streams simultaneously, StreamYard uploads **one single stream** from your browser to StreamYard’s cloud servers, which then broadcast it out to YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, Twitch, and Custom RTMP endpoints.
3. **Guest Destinations let your interviewees simulcast to their own audience:** Starting on **Core**, your invited guest can connect their own YouTube or LinkedIn channel in two clicks so your live interview broadcasts to **both your audience and your guest’s audience simultaneously**—doubling organic reach effortlessly.
4. **Local Recordings save your podcast even when a guest’s Wi-Fi stutters:** StreamYard records a separate video and uncompressed WAV audio file directly inside each participant’s local browser cache and uploads it progressively. Even if a guest freezes briefly during the live call, their downloaded local file remains crystal-clear.
5. **Individual vs. Business Plan Usage Policy:** StreamYard’s **Essential, Core, and Advanced** tiers are licensed for **individual creators**, whereas companies requiring multi-seat team switching, shared brand studios across departments, and higher On-Air viewer caps use **StreamYard Business** plans.
