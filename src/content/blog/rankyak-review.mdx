RankYak is an end-to-end AI SEO automation platform designed to take over the repetitive stages of organic growth: discovering keywords, structuring a topical calendar, generating long-form articles, publishing directly to your CMS, exchanging backlinks, and monitoring existing pages for ranking decay.

Developed by **Height Labs** in the Netherlands and having officially launched **version 2.0 in early September 2026**, RankYak targets solo founders, content bloggers, and small-to-mid businesses looking to build search authority without staffing an in-house content team.

This independent review evaluates how RankYak performs, details the true cost per article across various posting cadences, and analyzes whether its connected pipeline justifies its $99/month price tag.

## Security & Privacy: Data Handling & SEO Risks

Connecting an automated software platform to your website’s publishing engine and Search Console requires careful risk assessment:

### Technical & Organizational Security
- **EU Jurisdiction & GDPR:** Operated by Height Labs in the Netherlands, ensuring data handling falls under European Union privacy protections.
- **Access Scope:** RankYak requires administrative or contributor API access to your CMS to publish posts, as well as read access to your Google Search Console property.
- **Enterprise Deficits:** We found no public verification of SOC 2 Type 2 attestation, ISO 27001 certification, or SAML/SSO authentication.
- **Best Practice:** We recommend granting RankYak a dedicated CMS role with “Editor” or “Author” privileges rather than global Super Admin rights, and utilizing “Draft Mode” for initial deployments.

### Search Engine & Algorithmic Risks
- **Google’s Helpful Content Guidance:** Google’s official guidelines state that AI-assisted content is acceptable provided it offers genuine value to users. However, mass-producing thin, generic content without editorial oversight can violate Google’s scaled content abuse policies.
- **Reciprocal Link Networks:** RankYak’s backlink exchange pairs sites within a closed network. Google’s spam guidelines caution against excessive reciprocal link exchanges designed solely to manipulate PageRank. We advise treating the backlink exchange as a supplemental signal rather than your primary authority-building strategy.

While standard SEO tools abandon content once published, RankYak monitors post-launch performance:

- **Decay Refreshes:** The Pro plan automatically rewrites and refreshes underperforming articles on their original URLs.
- **Pricing Note:** While basic article refreshes are included in Pro, the complete Site Guard suite (Page Guard + Cannibalization Guard) is a **$99/month per site add-on**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is RankYak?
RankYak is an autonomous AI SEO software platform that handles keyword discovery, topical planning, article writing, direct CMS publishing, backlink exchange, and ranking decay optimization.

### 2. Is there a free plan available?
No. RankYak does not have a permanent free plan. However, they offer a **3-day free trial** with full platform capabilities for one website.

### 3. How much does RankYak cost?
RankYak Pro costs **$99 per website per month**, or **$82.50 per month ($990/year)** when billed annually. The Site Guard add-on is an additional $99 per site per month.

### 4. Can RankYak articles publish automatically to my website?
Yes. You can configure RankYak to publish directly to your CMS on a preset schedule (up to 1 article per day), or keep generated articles in **Draft Mode** for editorial approval.

### 5. Does RankYak support languages other than English?
Yes. RankYak supports content generation across **40+ languages**, including Vietnamese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Japanese.

### 6. Will Google penalize websites using RankYak?
Google’s official policy states that AI-assisted content is evaluated on helpfulness and quality, not production method. However, publishing hundreds of unreviewed AI articles without unique value can trigger Google's scaled content abuse spam penalties. Reviewing drafts and adding original insights is strongly recommended.

### 7. Does RankYak work with Shopify and WordPress?
Yes. RankYak provides an official WordPress plugin and direct integrations with Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Ghost, and Duda.

### 8. How does RankYak compare with Outrank?
Both platforms cost $99/month. RankYak provides deeper keyword authority scoring, Site Guard decay tracking, and an official MCP server, whereas Outrank focuses on flat organization pricing.

### 9. Can I manage multiple client websites on one account?
Yes. RankYak offers an Agency Partner option at $99 per client site per month, supporting up to 25 active domains under its fair-use terms.

### 10. What is RankYak’s cancellation and refund policy?
You can cancel at any time during the 3-day free trial without being billed. For paid subscription cycles, cancellations stop future renewal charges, but fees already paid are generally non-refundable per Height Labs' standard terms of service.
