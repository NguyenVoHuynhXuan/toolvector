## What Is ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs is an AI audio platform focused on generating, transforming, and deploying speech and other types of audio. Its current product ecosystem includes ElevenCreative, ElevenAgents, and ElevenAPI.

ElevenCreative is focused on content creation, ElevenAgents is designed for conversational AI agents, and ElevenAPI provides developer access to ElevenLabs’ AI capabilities. The platform is best known for its AI voice technology, particularly text-to-speech and voice cloning. However, the product has expanded considerably beyond basic text-to-speech.

Depending on the plan and product, users can access features including:
- Text to Speech
- Speech to Text

- Voice Changer
- Voice Design
- Voice Isolator
- Dubbing
- AI Music
- Sound Effects
- Image & Video
- Studio
- Voice Agents
- APIs

This makes ElevenLabs more of an AI audio and creative platform than simply an AI voice generator.

## How Does ElevenLabs Work?

The basic workflow is straightforward. You provide text, select or create a voice, adjust the available settings, and generate the audio.

For example, someone creating a YouTube video could write a script, select an AI voice, generate the narration, and then use the resulting audio in their production.

The platform provides thousands of voices and supports multiple languages. ElevenLabs’ current AI voice generator page advertises access to more than 10,000 voices across 70+ languages. For developers, ElevenLabs also provides APIs for integrating capabilities such as text-to-speech, speech-to-text, voice cloning, voice changer, and conversational AI into applications.

## ElevenLabs Voice Cloning

Voice cloning is one of the features that has helped make ElevenLabs particularly well known. Users can create a digital replica of a voice from audio samples.

ElevenLabs currently offers Instant Voice Cloning and Professional Voice Cloning. Instant Voice Cloning is designed for quickly creating a replica from a relatively short sample, while Professional Voice Cloning uses more extensive training audio for higher-fidelity results. Professional Voice Cloning requires a Creator plan or above.

ElevenLabs also uses voice-captcha technology to verify that Professional Voice Clones are created from the user’s own voice samples. This is an important distinction. Voice cloning can be extremely powerful, but it also raises obvious questions around consent and misuse. ElevenLabs has therefore implemented verification measures for professional voice cloning.

## Create Your Own AI Voice

You don’t always need to clone an existing voice. ElevenLabs also provides Voice Design, which allows users to create a new voice by describing the desired characteristics in a text prompt.

For example, a creator might describe the type of character, tone, age, accent, or speaking style they want. This can be particularly useful for:
- Fictional characters
- Games
- Animations
- Storytelling
- Creative projects
- Brand concepts

Voice Design is essentially another way of creating a voice when the exact voice you’re looking for isn’t available in the Voice Library.

## ElevenLabs Pricing

One of the biggest questions before signing up is pricing. At the time of this review, ElevenLabs offers several subscription levels.

- **Free ($0/month)**: Includes 10,000 credits/month, Text to Speech, Speech to Text, Sound Effects, Voice Design, Music, Productions, Image, 3 Studio projects.
- **Starter ($6/month)**: Includes Free features plus Commercial License, Instant Voice Cloning, 30,000 credits/month, 20 Studio projects, Dubbing Studio, Image & Video.
- **Creator ($22/month)**: Includes 121,000 credits/month and Professional Voice Cloning. (Currently displaying a first-month promotional price of $11).
- **Pro ($99/month)**: Includes 600,000 credits/month, higher audio quality options (44.1kHz PCM audio output via API, 192kbps quality audio).
- **Scale ($299/month)**: Includes 1.8 million credits/month, 3 workspace seats, team collaboration, 3 Professional Voice Clones.
- **Business ($990/month)**: Includes 6 million credits/month, 10 workspace seats, 10 Professional Voice Clones, lower-latency text-to-speech options.
