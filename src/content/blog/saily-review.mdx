Saily is a prepaid travel eSIM application developed by **Nord Security**, the cybersecurity firm behind NordVPN, NordPass, and NordLocker. Since its public launch in early 2024, Saily has expanded from a straightforward data-plan shop into an integrated travel utility offering built-in web protection, an optional US phone number add-on, and a high-tier travel subscription called **Saily Ultra**.

However, behind its slick marketing and security branding lies an essential reality: Saily’s standard plans are **data-only**, its “unlimited” tiers have daily speed caps, and for larger data packages or multi-country European itineraries, primary rivals like **Airalo** often provide cheaper rates and wider country coverage.

This research-based evaluation examines how Saily performs in practice, breaks down real-world pricing across Europe and Asia, evaluates its built-in security features, and highlights the crucial gotchas to consider before boarding your flight.

## 5 Things to Know Before Buying Saily

1. **Standard plans are strictly data-only:** Saily plans do not include cellular voice minutes or standard SMS text messaging. To receive SMS or place calls, you must purchase a separate **US (+1) phone number add-on** for $0.99/month plus additional calling/text bundles.
2. **Small plans are cheap, but large plans are not necessarily cheaper:** While Saily offers aggressive entry pricing on 1 GB plans ($3.99 in Spain or Japan, and as low as $1.99 in Austria/Czechia), Nord Security’s own published comparisons acknowledge that Airalo is frequently cheaper or comparable on 5 GB, 10 GB, and 20 GB tiers.
3. **Rigid validity periods per data tier:** Saily locks plan duration to data size. For example, 1 GB plans are strictly valid for 7 days, while 3 GB to 20 GB plans are valid for 30 days. You cannot buy a 3-day emergency pass like Airalo offers, nor can you extend a 1 GB plan across a month-long trip without purchasing a larger tier.
4. **“Unlimited” data comes with daily throttling:** Saily’s unlimited plans provide **5 GB per day at full 4G/5G speeds**. Once you exceed 5 GB within a 24-hour cycle, speed is throttled to approximately **1 Mbps** until midnight local time (sufficient for WhatsApp text and Google Maps, but sluggish for HD video).
5. **Connection issues upon landing are usually device-level settings:** According to user troubleshooting reports, the most frequent reason travelers experience “No Internet” upon landing is failing to switch mobile data to the Saily eSIM line or forgetting to enable **Data Roaming** on Android devices.

## What Is Saily?

Saily is an international prepaid mobile data eSIM service operated by **Nord Security**. Legally, the consumer service operates through **Saily UAB** (headquartered in Vilnius, Lithuania) and **CyberQuay Pty Ltd** (Sydney, Australia), with **Saily Inc.** designated as the primary data controller in its official privacy policy (last updated June 8, 2026).

- **Feature Score:** **4.4/5**

### 3. Fixed GB Plans vs. Capped Unlimited Data

Saily structures its catalogue around transparent gigabyte tiers, accompanied by an “Unlimited” option for high-volume travelers.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Is Saily safe and legitimate?
Yes. Saily is developed by Nord Security (the parent company of NordVPN) and operates through European legal entities in Lithuania. Its built-in DNS threat protection shields devices from malicious URLs and ad trackers.

### 2. Can Saily make phone calls and send text messages?
Standard Saily data packages do not include cellular minutes or SMS. However, you can add a dedicated **US (+1) phone number** in the app for $0.99/month and purchase supplementary calling/SMS bundles.

### 3. How much does Saily cost?
Prices start between **$1.99 and $3.99 for 1 GB** (7 days validity) in popular destinations. 10 GB / 30-day packages generally range from $15.99 to $20.99, while the Saily Ultra subscription costs $59.99/month.

### 4. Does Saily support personal hotspot tethering?
Yes. Saily permits **unlimited personal hotspot sharing** across all standard GB-based data packages, provided your smartphone hardware supports tethering.

### 5. Saily vs. Airalo: Which is better?
Saily is superior for short trips where 7-day 1 GB plans and built-in ad blocking are prioritized. Airalo is better for multi-country European travel and high-volume data packages (10 GB to 20 GB).

### 6. Is the Saily Ultra subscription worth $59.99/month?
Saily Ultra is worth it only for frequent international travelers who actively utilize the included **airport lounge passes, fast-track perks, and NordVPN/NordPass licenses**. Occasional tourists will spend significantly less with standard pay-as-you-go plans.

### 7. How does Saily’s refund policy work?
Saily provides a **full refund within 30 days of purchase**, provided the eSIM profile was never installed or the data plan was never activated.

### 8. What should I do if my Saily eSIM is not working after landing?
Ensure your phone’s mobile data is set to the Saily eSIM line, and confirm that **Data Roaming is toggled ON** in your cellular settings (particularly on Android devices).

### 9. Does Saily include a full VPN?
Not quite. Saily includes a **Virtual Location changer** (115+ locations) that reroutes DNS requests and spoofs your IP address. However, it does not encrypt all device traffic with the military-grade tunneling protocol of the standalone NordVPN application.

### 10. How many destinations does Saily cover?
According to Saily’s official website, the service provides cellular connectivity across **200+ countries and global territories**.
