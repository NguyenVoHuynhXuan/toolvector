### 1. 300+ Criteria Website Audit & Technical Crawler

*Seobility Website Audit: granular on-page health scoring across Technical & Meta, Structure, and Content layers, paired with page-level issue prioritization.*

Website Auditing is the crown jewel of Seobility—and the reason industry veterans like Brian Dean and Matt Diggity have long praised its diagnostic clarity. Every crawl evaluates **more than 300 technical, structural, and content signals** across up to **25,000 pages/project (Premium)**, **50,000 pages (Professional)**, or **100,000 pages (Agency)** using 3 to 5 parallel crawler instances.

Key diagnostic checks include:
- **Tech & Meta Layer:** HTTP status codes, server timeouts, meta title/description pixel lengths, canonical tag mismatches, `hreflang` validation, JavaScript rendering execution, HTML response times, and Google Safe Browsing / blacklist status.
- **Site Architecture & Internal Linking:** Click-distance calculation from the homepage, orphan or buried money pages, internal links pointing to redirecting URLs (redirect chains), anchor-text distribution, and up to **50,000 external outbound links** checked per crawl.
- **Deep Content & Cannibalization Diagnostics:** Unlike basic crawlers that only flag identical `<title>` tags, Seobility detects **duplicate paragraphs reused across pages**, thin-content URLs, title-to-body topical mismatch, **keyword cannibalization**, and even **common spelling typos**.
- **AI Search Bot Access Check (GEO):** Audits your `robots.txt` directives to verify whether AI search crawlers—specifically **OAI-SearchBot** (ChatGPT Search), **PerplexityBot**, and **Claude-SearchBot**—can access or are inadvertently blocked from indexing your key pages.
