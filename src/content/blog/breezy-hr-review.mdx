## Quick Verdict

Breezy HR is one of the most approachable applicant tracking systems (ATS) for small and mid-sized companies. It pairs a visual drag-and-drop pipeline with unlimited users on every plan, a genuinely usable free tier, and a 2026 AI layer (Breezy Intelligence) that explains its scores instead of hiding them.

**Buy it if** you run hiring inside a company of roughly 10 to 250 people, want hiring managers to use the tool without training, and want to pay a flat monthly fee rather than per seat.

**Skip it if** you need enterprise-grade reporting, deep audit trails, public API access without a custom quote, or a documented data-hosting region outside the US and EU.

**Biggest strength:** ease of use plus transparent, pay-as-you-go AI. **Biggest weakness:** the best features (offers, HRIS sync, candidate comparison) sit on the $439/month plan, and AI costs extra.

### 5 Things to Know Before Buying

1. **Pricing is flat, not per user.** Every plan includes unlimited users. A three-person HR team and a ten-person team pay the same.
2. **The free plan is real but narrow.** Bootstrap allows one active position or candidate pool at a time. Everything else can be archived, not deleted.
3. **Premium features are tier-gated.** Scorecards and e-signatures start at Growth ($273/month billed annually). Offer management, HRIS integrations and candidate comparison start at Business ($439/month billed annually).
4. **AI is an add-on, priced by usage.** Breezy Intelligence credits start at $30 per 100,000 and never expire. Scoring one candidate uses about 500 credits.
5. **Plans auto-renew.** The Terms of Service say subscriptions renew for a term of the same length unless you cancel before the renewal date. Confirm refund rules with sales before paying annually.

## What Is Breezy HR?
<Image src={pic0} alt="Aimfox Review" class="prose-img" />
Breezy HR is a cloud-based applicant tracking system built around a visual pipeline. You post jobs to a branded career site and to 50+ job boards, move candidates through customizable stages, automate screening and scheduling, and then extend offers and hand new hires to your HRIS. The company says more than 17,000 organizations use it.

It is owned by Learning Technologies Group plc (LTG), a UK-listed company that acquired Breezy in 2019, which matters for security and contract terms because LTG's compliance program covers Breezy. The product line has three parts: Breezy Hire (the ATS, our focus), Breezy Onboard (onboarding workflows, from $49/month) and Breezy Perform (performance management, sold separately).

**Best for:** small and mid-sized businesses, startups, agencies and HR generalists who hire without a large recruiting team.

**Not ideal for:** large enterprises with complex compliance, global data-residency demands, or analytics teams that need custom BI out of the box.

## Key Features: The Deep Dive

Breezy organizes its product into three stages: attract, qualify and hire. We spend the most time where the product has changed most: automation and the Breezy Intelligence AI suite.

### 1. Job distribution and career sites (Attract)
<Image src={pic1} alt="Aimfox Review" class="prose-img" />

**What it does:** Breezy publishes a role to 50+ job boards from one screen and creates a mobile-friendly, branded career page. A separate employee-referral portal imports your open roles automatically. Premium job sites let you target audiences for specific positions.

**How it works in practice:** you write a job once, choose the boards, and applicants from every source flow into the same pipeline with the source tracked. That source data feeds the built-in "Candidate Sources" report, so you can see which boards actually produce hires. Breezy's own 2026 Source of Hire Report headlines that 76% of hires come from a single source, a vendor-published figure worth reading as a prompt to check your own data rather than as a universal truth.

**Strengths:**
- Career site and multi-board distribution are included even on the free Bootstrap plan.
- Multi-language configuration for the platform and career page.
- Custom domain for your career portal starts on Startup.

**Limits:**
- The free plan allows only one active position at a time.
- Premium boards are positioned as targeted upgrades.
- Employee referrals and the "Hello" messenger for passive candidates start at Growth.

**Compared with rivals:** a free plan with distribution to dozens of boards is uncommon. JazzHR lists no free plan at all.

**Who benefits most:** teams hiring occasionally who need to be visible fast, and startups that cannot yet justify a paid ATS.

### 2. Visual pipeline and candidate profiles
<Image src={pic3} alt="Aimfox Review" class="prose-img" />

**What it does:** candidates sit in a Kanban-style board you can customize to your process. Each profile combines the resume, social links, team notes and message history, and from the same screen you can email or text the candidate, schedule an interview, complete a scorecard, send an assessment or background check, and issue an offer.

**Example workflow:** a candidate applies, receives an automatic acknowledgment, lands in "New," and a recruiter drags them to "Phone Screen." The move triggers a self-scheduling link, and the hiring manager gets a Slack notification. Nobody opens a spreadsheet.

**Strengths:**
- Ease of use is the most consistent theme across independent reviews. New hiring managers can be productive on day one without training.
- Native iOS and Android apps, Slack integration, bulk actions (Startup and up), and unified email and text history.
- SMS is an add-on from $41/month, so texting is not free.

**Limits:**
- Multiple, role-specific pipelines require Growth. Startup gives you one customizable pipeline.
- Resume search, parsing and multi-candidate review could be smoother according to some user feedback.

**Who benefits most:** non-recruiters. Operations managers and founders who hire occasionally get the biggest lift from a tool that does not demand HR expertise.

### 3. Automation and touchless pre-screening

**What it does:** Stage actions fire when a candidate enters a pipeline stage. They can send messages, questionnaires and assessments, request team feedback, and schedule interviews. Questionnaires turn the application form into a screening tool that advances or disqualifies candidates based on answers. You can also add an AI score threshold so strong applicants advance automatically. Nurture campaigns (Business) send timed email sequences to candidate pools.

**Example workflow:** a retail operations manager role requires weekend availability and a driver's license. Two knockout questions auto-disqualify applicants who answer "no." Everyone else receives a one-way video prompt. When they submit it, they move to "Review," and the hiring manager receives a task. Total recruiter time before a human decision: close to zero.

**Strengths:**
- Included from the Startup plan, not locked behind enterprise pricing.
- Combines rule-based logic (questionnaires) with AI scoring in one flow.

**Limits:**
- Complexity grows with configuration. Plan an afternoon of setup rather than ten minutes.
- Advanced multi-path questionnaires are a Business feature.

**Who benefits most:** high-volume hiring for roles like hospitality, retail, sales and support, where the first filter is factual rather than judgment-based.

### 4. Breezy Intelligence: the AI suite (Deep Dive)
<Image src={pic4} alt="Aimfox Review" class="prose-img" />
This is the part of Breezy that has changed the most. Breezy Intelligence is an add-on available on every plan, including the free one, and it runs on credits.

**Applicant Insights (AI scoring).** Applicant Insights reads each application against your job description and up to five categories you control, then returns a 0 to 10 score with a category-by-category explanation. Breezy drafts the categories from your job post, and you edit them. Personal details are anonymized before scoring, and the criteria themselves receive a compliance check. Questionnaire answers can count toward the score. You can test criteria on a sample before scoring the whole pool; that test uses about 100 credits. Each candidate review uses about 500 credits.

Two design choices stand out:
1. AI scores cannot move anyone into a disqualified stage, which keeps a human in the rejection decision.
2. Breezy states that Warden AI, an independent assurance provider, audits Applicant Insights monthly for sex, race/ethnicity and intersectional bias, and that it completed a New York City Local Law 144 bias audit.

**Resume Audit (fake and AI-written resume detection).** Resume Audit inspects how a resume was created, when it was created and how closely its wording mirrors your job post, then labels it on a scale from "looks original" to "likely has copied or AI-generated content." It flags resumes from generators and bots, resumes created the same day someone applied, copy-and-paste jobs and keyword stuffing. A scan typically uses up to 600 credits depending on resume length.

*Our analysis:* AI-text detection is probabilistic. Treat a flag as a reason to look closer, not as proof, and avoid auto-rejecting solely on it. Breezy's Terms of Service state AI output should not be the sole basis for a hiring decision.

**Candidate Email Verification and spam filtering.** Every new applicant receives a code by email, and anyone who does not confirm moves to a separate stage. Spam filtering also flags disposable email addresses.

**Activity Summary.** Condenses emails, notes, interview highlights and stage changes into a short summary with suggested next steps.

**Interview Analysis.** Scores recorded interviews against criteria your team writes, and combines multiple interviews into one overall score. This is a separate subscription.

**Breezy MCP Server.** The Model Context Protocol (MCP) server connects Breezy to AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor. You can ask an assistant to pull a candidate's scorecards, move candidates between stages, add notes, or send messages following your existing permissions.

**Strengths:**
- Transparent scoring with explanations, plus anonymization and a bias-audit process.
- Pay-as-you-go credits that never expire, with unlimited users.
- Available on the free plan, letting small teams test AI screening without upgrading.

**Limits:**
- Usage-based pricing requires monitoring for high-volume hiring.
- Interview Analysis is a separate subscription.

### 5. Interviews, scheduling and team collaboration
<Image src={pic5} alt="Aimfox Review" class="prose-img" />

**What it does:** interviewers' calendars (Google and Outlook) appear side by side, and you drag interviews into open slots. Candidates can self-schedule from your real availability. Native browser-based video interviews show the resume, scorecard and live notes on one screen, and you can also use Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams. One-way video lets candidates record answers on their own time.

**Structured interviewing (Growth and up):** customizable interview guides tell each interviewer what to ask, scorecards produce apples-to-apples comparisons, and Business adds side-by-side candidate comparison and recording of integrated video interviews.

**Strengths:**
- Self-scheduling and team availability views remove most scheduling emails.
- Structured guides and scorecards help reduce bias and inconsistency.

**Limits:**
- Structured interviewing starts at Growth, not Startup.
- Occasional calendar sync delays reported by some users; test during your trial.

**Who benefits most:** companies where hiring managers conduct interviews without recruiter support.

### 6. Offers, e-signatures, background checks, HRIS and compliance (Hire)

**What it does:** Business-plan customers get offer templates, approval flows and built-in e-signature for offers, plus HRIS integrations to push new hires into payroll systems (BambooHR, Gusto, Rippling, etc.). Background checks run through integrated providers (available from Startup). E-signatures for documents like NDAs start at Growth. EEOC and OFCCP data tracking is a toggle from Startup, and GDPR tools include candidate consent collection.

**Strengths:**
- The full loop from application to offer is covered without a second tool.
- Compliance tracking is included at entry-level paid tiers.

**Limits:**
- Offer management and HRIS integrations require the Business plan ($439/month annually).
- Compliance and reporting are lighter than dedicated enterprise ATS tools.

**Who benefits most:** US-based SMBs that need compliance basics and a clean handoff to payroll.

### 7. Reporting, analytics and add-on products

**What it does:** out-of-the-box dashboards cover pipeline performance, candidate sources, time to fill and team productivity. Ad hoc dashboards, custom KPIs, API access, and data export/import are positioned as Custom Pro add-ons. Breezy Onboard (from $49/month) creates role-based onboarding workflows, and Breezy Perform handles reviews, goals and 1:1s.

**Strengths:** built-in reports answer core questions about which sources and stages work.

**Limits:** advanced reporting requires a Custom Pro quote.

## Security & Privacy

- **Certifications (group level):** Breezy HR is covered by LTG's security program, which lists ISO 27001:2022, ISO 27701:2019, SOC 2 Type II reports (under NDA), UK ICO registration, and Data Privacy Framework compliance.
- **GDPR and data location:** EU and UK customers get EU data centers, candidate consent tools, and GDPR tooling. Where Breezy acts as a processor, LTG's Data Protection Addendum applies.
- **Access controls:** Multi-factor authentication from Startup; custom roles start at Business; SAML SSO is available on Custom Pro plans.
- **AI and data use:** Breezy's Terms state customer content is not used to train AI models, and Applicant Insights anonymizes personal data before scoring.
- **Contract and liability:** Florida law governs disputes; liability is capped at the greater of $20 or the fees paid in the prior three months.
- **Support hours:** Weekdays from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern time, with a target response within 24 hours.
